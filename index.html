About

I am a second-year undergraduate at the University of Toronto, completing a double Specialist in Computer Science and Data Science.

I like working with messy real-world data and turning it into models whose results I can explain. My latest project, TTC Subway Delay Prediction, predicts long subway delays from 45,000+ Toronto Open Data records, from cleaning inconsistent station names to tuning classifiers and checking how performance holds up over time.

I am most interested in applied machine learning, and I am taking Machine Learning (CSC311) and Statistical Inference (STA261) in Winter 2027. I am looking for research opportunities for Summer 2027 — feel free to reach me at steven.zou@mail.utoronto.ca.
