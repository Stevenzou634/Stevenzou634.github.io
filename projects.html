TTC Subway Delay Prediction

2026 – Present
Personal Project · GitHub

Predicting whether a Toronto subway delay will be long (5 minutes or more) from 45,000+ TTC delay records published on Toronto Open Data.

  • Consolidated 1,251 inconsistent station names into 86 categories and excluded a target-leaking feature (Min Gap, r = 0.83 with delay length).
  • Built logistic regression and random forest classifiers with a time-based split; tuning the decision threshold on a validation period raised F1 from 0.38 to 0.48, beating class weighting (0.44) with half the false alarms.
  • Found that infrastructure failures cause 55% of long delays at 5–6 am versus 14% at other times, and identified a 2025–2026 shift in delay rates.

Electricity Access in African Countries

2026
STA130 Course Project

Analyzed data from 54 African countries to study how electrification relates to poverty, population density, HDI, electricity cost and per-capita consumption. Using exploratory analysis and linear regression, found a negative association between electrification and poverty rates, with low-density countries showing lower access — informing potential indicators for the Clean Air Task Force (CATF) electricity sector scorecard.
